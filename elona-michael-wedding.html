For out-of-towners

Getting there

There are three handy airports for Kosovo — Prishtina (PRN), Skopje in North Macedonia (SKP) and Tirana in Albania (TIA). Our tip: fly into Prishtina and home from Skopje. Here's why, and a few other ways to plan it.

FlightsFrom London
  • To Prishtina (PRN) — Wizz Air from Luton. Only Luton flies here, and the return lands back in Luton at around 2am — not the nicest arrival.
  • To Skopje (SKP) — Wizz Air from Luton. The same-day return leaves Skopje at ~7:50pm and lands in Luton around 10:10pm — a much friendlier time.
  • To Tirana (TIA) — plenty of flights, including Wizz Air from Luton and Ryanair from Stansted.
Our tipFly in, fly out
  • Into Prishtina, home from Skopje — because the Prishtina return lands at ~2am but the Skopje return gets you home for a reasonable 10pm, we suggest flying directly into Prishtina and returning from Skopje.
  • Skopje transfer — a taxi from Prishtina to Skopje airport takes about 1.5 hours and costs roughly €65–85. If we see several of you on the same flight, we'll ask whether you'd like to share the drive.
  • Prefer Albania? — you can do the same via Tirana. The drive is a bit longer (about 3 hours to Prishtina) but the views are gorgeous, with lovely stops along the way, especially in northern Albania.
On wheelsRenting a car
  • Pick up in one city, drop off in another — handy if you're flying into one airport and out of another. You can collect the car in Skopje, Tirana or Prishtina and return it in a different one.
  • Prishtina–Tirana Rent a Car →
  • Great for exploring Kosovo and northern Albania at your own pace on the way in or out.
On arrivalFrom the airport
  • The venue is close — Aviano Restaurant is only a 5 minute drive from the airport, so it's easy if you're arriving or leaving on the day.
  • Taxi — about 20–25 min to the city centre; agree the fare first (usually €15–25).
  • Hotel transfers — many Prishtina hotels arrange airport pickup.
Good to knowPractical notes
  • Currency — Kosovo uses the Euro (€).
  • Entry — UK passport holders don't need a visa for short stays. Check your passport validity before flying.
  • Getting around — taxis are cheap and plentiful; the centre is very walkable.
Where to stayAccommodation
  • Stay central — hotels in Prishtina are reasonably priced and usually lovely. We'd suggest somewhere central: more to do day and night, and plenty of restaurants on your doorstep.
  • Hotel Prishtina — central and comfortable, in the heart of the city.
  • Emery Hotel — a smart 4-star just a few minutes' walk from the Newborn Monument.
  • Grand Boutique Hotel — a lovely, highly-rated boutique spot with a garden and private parking.

Make the most of it

Things to do

If you're travelling all this way, stay a few extra days — Kosovo is warm, affordable and full of surprises.

In the cityPrishtina
  • Mother Teresa Boulevard — the lively pedestrian heart of the city, perfect for a coffee and people-watching.
  • Newborn Monument — the iconic typographic landmark marking Kosovo's independence.
  • National Library of Kosovo — one of the most striking buildings in the Balkans.
  • Café culture — Prishtina takes its coffee seriously; the macchiato scene is legendary.
Beyond the cityDay trips
  • Prizren — Kosovo's most beautiful old town: Ottoman bridge, hillside fortress, cobbled streets (about 1.5 hrs away).
  • Rugova Canyon & Peja — dramatic mountains, hiking and the Patriarchate monastery.
  • Gjakova & Gracanica — historic bazaars and UNESCO-listed monasteries.
  • Brezovica — mountain scenery in the Sharr Mountains.
At the tableEat & drink
  • Flia — layered traditional pastry, a must-try.
  • Grilled meats — qebapa, pleskavica and fresh bread everywhere.
  • Local wine & rakia — the Rahovec wine region is well worth a visit.
Little thingsTips
  • Language — Albanian is the main language; English is widely spoken by younger people.
  • Weather in April — mild spring days, cooler evenings; bring a light jacket.
  • Hospitality — Kosovars are famously welcoming; don't be surprised by an extra coffee invitation.
